# NNNN. Short title describing the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation? What forced a decision to be made? Include the constraints that
actually mattered — cost, existing systems, what is already running elsewhere, what has to
keep working.

Facts only. Do not argue for the decision here.

## Decision

What was chosen. One or two sentences, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

For each one: what it was, and the specific reason it was not chosen.

- **Alternative A** — why not.
- **Alternative B** — why not.

If an alternative was close, say that. A decision recorded as obvious when it was not will be
re-opened by someone who sees the trade-off that was left out.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, and what it makes harder or impossible. The second half is the part
people skip and the part that is worth reading two years later.

Include anything that now has to be remembered — a manual step, a limit that will be hit
eventually, a cost that recurs.
